bio
I am a Project Researcher at the University of Turku and Turku PET Centre, with a solid background in Mathematics and Computer Science. My research focuses on machine learning, physiological signal and image processing, biomedical time-series analysis, and mathematical methods for biomedical applications. I have practical experience in privacy-preserving AI and federated learning, and my work has mainly involved ECG and other physiological signals, medical imaging, and dynamic cardiac PET. I currently contribute to AI4HOPE and to dynamic cardiac PET motion analysis and correction. I am particularly interested in combining mathematical methods with machine learning to address practical problems in biomedical research.
